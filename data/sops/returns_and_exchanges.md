# SOP — Returns and Exchanges queue

Scope: product returns, exchanges, refunds for purchased goods.

## Triage
1. Confirm order number, purchase date, and whether the item is within the return window.
2. Determine reason (defect, wrong item, unwanted) — defects may qualify for warranty instead.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Within policy: issue the RMA, share the return label/portal steps and refund timeline.
2. Defective items: offer repair/replace/refund per warranty terms.
3. On receipt of the return, trigger the refund/exchange and confirm completion.

## Escalation
Escalate to the returns supervisor for out-of-window requests or high-value exceptions.
