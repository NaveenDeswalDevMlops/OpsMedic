# SOP — Service Outages and Maintenance queue

Scope: reported outages, degraded service, and questions about maintenance windows.

## Triage
1. Match the report to any active incident/maintenance; if none, verify service health immediately.
2. Record impact scope (features, regions, user counts) and start time.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Active incident: link the ticket, share status-page updates and ETA; notify on restore.
2. New outage: declare an incident, engage the on-call team, post initial comms within SLA.
3. Maintenance queries: share the schedule, expected impact, and preparation guidance.

## Escalation
Escalate to the incident commander for P1/P2 outages or SLA-breaching restore times.
