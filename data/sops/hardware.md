# SOP — Hardware (laptops, desktops, printers, docks, peripherals)

## Triage checklist
1. Capture asset tag, model, and exact symptom (no power, no display, device not detected, physical damage).
2. Determine business impact and whether a loaner is needed immediately.
3. Check warranty/AMC status for RMA eligibility.

## Standard resolution steps
1. Power issues: known-good charger + outlet; hard reset (hold power 30s); reseat battery if removable.
2. Display/dock issues: update dock firmware and graphics/USB-C drivers; swap cable; isolate with a known-good dock.
3. Printers: restart spooler on the print server, clear stuck jobs, power-cycle, reseat network cable, test page.
4. If hardware fault confirmed: issue loaner from stock, raise vendor RMA, track replacement to closure.

## Escalation
Escalate to Field Services for physical repairs/site visits; to Vendor RMA desk for warranty claims.
