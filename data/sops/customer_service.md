# SOP — Customer Service queue

Scope: general customer inquiries, account questions, service information requests.

## Triage
1. Restate the customer's ask in one line; verify account ownership before sharing account data.
2. Route misfiled technical/billing items to their proper queue with context.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Answer from the knowledge base/product documentation; link the source article.
2. For account changes, follow identity verification, apply the change, and confirm in writing.
3. Log the interaction summary so future similar tickets retrieve it.

## Escalation
Escalate to a team lead for complaints, refunds beyond policy, or legal/compliance topics.
