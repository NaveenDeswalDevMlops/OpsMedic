# SOP — Technical Support queue

Scope: technical issues with software, devices, connectivity, and integrations reported by customers/users.

## Triage
1. Capture product/system, exact error text, when it started, and recent changes (updates, config).
2. Classify severity by business impact; check for a matching known incident before individual debugging.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Reproduce or confirm the symptom; gather logs/screenshots.
2. Apply the closest known fix from similar resolved tickets (version update, config rollback, cache clear, driver/firmware update).
3. Verify with the user; document exact steps taken in the ticket.

## Escalation
Escalate to L2/engineering with logs when the fix is not covered by prior resolutions or requires code/infra changes.
