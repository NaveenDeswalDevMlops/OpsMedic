# SOP — Sales and Pre-Sales queue

Scope: pricing questions, product capability inquiries, quotes, trials.

## Triage
1. Qualify the request: product(s) of interest, scale/seat count, timeline.
2. Check for an existing account/opportunity to route to the owning rep.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Send standard pricing/feature collateral for straightforward asks.
2. For custom needs, gather requirements and open an opportunity for the sales team with full context.
3. Trials: provision per the trial policy and schedule a follow-up.

## Escalation
Hand off to the account executive for negotiations, custom terms, or enterprise agreements.
