# SOP — Purchase (procurement of devices, licenses, accessories, software requests)

## Triage checklist
1. Confirm item, quantity, business justification, and cost-center/manager approval status.
2. Check the standard catalog first — non-catalog items need extra approval.
3. For software, check existing license pool for reclaimable seats before buying.

## Standard resolution steps
1. Catalog item with approval: raise the purchase order in procurement, share the PO/ETA with the requester.
2. License request: allocate from the pool if seats exist; otherwise add to the next true-up or raise a new PO per policy.
3. Non-catalog/new vendor: route through procurement vetting (security + finance) — set expectation of longer lead time.
4. On delivery: update the asset register/license inventory, then confirm closure with the requester.

## Escalation
Escalate to Procurement team for vendor negotiation, and to Software Asset Management for license compliance questions.
