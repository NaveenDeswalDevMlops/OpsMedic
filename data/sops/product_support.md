# SOP — Product Support queue

Scope: product-specific usage problems, compatibility questions, defect reports.

## Triage
1. Capture product model/version, environment (OS, firmware), and reproduction steps.
2. Check the known-issues list and release notes for the reported version first.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Known issue: share the documented workaround/fix version and link the advisory.
2. New issue: reproduce, collect diagnostics, apply standard remedies (update, reset, reconfigure).
3. Confirm resolution; if defect confirmed, file a product bug with diagnostics attached.

## Escalation
Escalate to the product engineering queue with reproduction steps for confirmed defects.
