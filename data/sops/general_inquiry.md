# SOP — General Inquiry queue

Scope: questions and requests that fit no other queue.

## Triage
1. Summarize the ask and confirm understanding with the requester.
2. Re-route to the correct queue if one clearly applies — with context, not a bare transfer.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Answer from the knowledge base where possible and link the article.
2. For new/unusual asks, identify the owning team, hand off, and track to closure.
3. If the same inquiry recurs, draft a KB article so retrieval answers it next time.

## Escalation
Escalate to the service desk lead when ownership is unclear after one hand-off.
