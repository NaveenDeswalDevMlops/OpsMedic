# SOP — Billing and Payments queue

Scope: invoice questions, payment failures, charge discrepancies, subscription billing.

## Triage
1. Verify account identity before discussing any billing data.
2. Collect invoice number, disputed amount/date, and payment method.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Explain charges against the plan/price list; correct obvious clerical errors directly.
2. Payment failures: check gateway status and card/mandate validity; retry or offer an alternate method.
3. Genuine discrepancies: raise a billing adjustment with finance and give the customer a reference and ETA.

## Escalation
Escalate to Finance for refunds beyond desk authority, chargebacks, or tax questions.
