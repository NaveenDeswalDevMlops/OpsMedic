# SOP — Human Resources queue

Scope: employee HR matters surfacing via the helpdesk (onboarding tech, HR portal, policy questions).

## Triage
1. Separate IT-side items (accounts, portal access) from HR-policy items (payroll, leave rules).
2. Verify the requester is the affected employee or authorized HR staff.

## Standard resolution flow
1. Portal/access issues: standard access SOP (verify, fix membership/SSO, confirm).
2. Onboarding/offboarding: execute the joiner/leaver checklist with dated evidence.
3. Policy/payroll questions: route to HR operations; never change HR data from the service desk.

## Escalation
Escalate to HRMS application support for system faults; to HR ops for any data or policy decision.
